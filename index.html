Our story

Durban recipes, Pretoria East kitchen

4 Chilli Indian Takeaway was founded in 2011 to fill a gap: there was no authentic, traditional Durban-style curry, masala or Indian spice in Pretoria East. We set out to cook it properly, with carefully roasted masalas and spices, fresh rotis, a variety of samoosas and, of course, the bunny chows.

Everything at 418 Jacqueline Drive is made to order. That takes a little longer, but it means every dish reaches you fresh, never from under a heat lamp. Our samoosas are fried and served immediately.

We also keep a steady stock of premium masalas, authentic spices and quality frozen foods, so the full range is always on the shelf.

  • 01 Cooked to order Nothing pre-plated, nothing reheated.
  • 02 Take away only WhatsApp or call ahead, then collect. We don't deliver.
  • 03 Vegan done properly Bean, dhall and mixed veg curries built on their own base.

More than a take away

A full Indian grocery store

The other half of the shop. Everything you need to cook the same food at home: pantry staples, fresh produce and the spice wall we cook from ourselves.
