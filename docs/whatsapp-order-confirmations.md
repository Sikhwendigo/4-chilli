# Confirming website orders on WhatsApp

Every order sent from the website starts with an order number, for example:

> Hi 4 Chilli, I'd like to place order **#1840-LR**:

The customer sees the same number on their screen and is told the kitchen will confirm it on WhatsApp. If they call, they'll quote that number.

## Confirm an order in two taps

1. **Swipe right on the customer's order message** (or long-press → Reply). Your reply then quotes their message, order number included, so you don't need to type it.
2. Type **`/`** and pick a quick reply from the list below, fill in the time, and send.

## Set up the quick replies (once)

In the **WhatsApp Business** app: **Settings → Business tools → Quick replies → +**. Add each of these with its shortcut:

| Shortcut | Message |
|---|---|
| `/ok` | ✅ Thanks! Your order is confirmed and will be ready for collection at ____. See you at 418 Jacqueline Drive, Garsfontein. – 4 Chilli |
| `/ready` | 🔥 Your order is ready! Come collect at 418 Jacqueline Drive, Garsfontein. – 4 Chilli |
| `/busy` | Thanks for your order! We're very busy right now, so it'll be about ____ minutes. Reply OK to keep it, or let us know if you'd like to cancel. |
| `/out` | Sorry, we're out of ____ today. Would you like ____ instead? We'll hold your order until you reply. |
| `/closed` | Sorry, the kitchen has closed for food orders today. We open again at ____. You're welcome to send your order then! |

## No WhatsApp Business yet?

Quick replies, labels and away messages only exist in the free **WhatsApp Business** app. To switch, keeping the same number (083 786 4410) and its chats:

1. In normal WhatsApp, back up your chats: **Settings → Chats → Chat backup**.
2. Install **WhatsApp Business** from the App Store or Play Store.
3. Enter the same number; it offers to move the account and chats across.
4. Add the shop name, address, hours and logo as the profile.

A number can only be on one of the two apps at a time, so if it's also your personal number, your personal chats move too.

**Staying on normal WhatsApp?** Use your phone keyboard's text shortcuts instead of quick replies. Type the shortcut and the full message appears:

- **iPhone:** Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement → **+**
- **Android (Gboard):** Settings → Dictionary → Personal dictionary → **+**

Use the messages from the table above, with shortcuts like `okk`, `rdyy`, `busyy`. Swipe-to-reply still quotes the order number. Labels and the automatic away message aren't available in normal WhatsApp.

## Keep track with labels (optional)

**Settings → Business tools → Labels**, then long-press a chat to label it:

- **New order**: just arrived, not yet answered
- **Confirmed**: you've sent `/ok`
- **Ready**: you've sent `/ready`
- **Collected**: done

The **New order** label shows at a glance which customers are still waiting.

## Automatic reply when you're closed (optional)

**Settings → Business tools → Away message**: turn it on and choose **Outside of business hours**, with a message like:

> Thanks for your message! We're closed right now and will reply when we open. Our hours are Mon–Sat 09:00–19:00 and Sun 10:00–17:00 (kitchen closes 30 min earlier).

That way customers who order after hours get an instant answer instead of silence.
